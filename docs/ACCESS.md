## OSF Projects phase-out

The Center for Open Science is retiring OSF Projects as a place to collaborate, manage files, and store data ([OSF changes](https://www.cos.io/osf-changes); [transition note](https://help.osf.io/article/727-osf-projects-transition); [API impact](https://help.osf.io/article/744-osf-projects-api-impact)). From 16 November 2026, new projects and child components cannot be created. From 19 February 2027, existing projects become read-only. Public projects remain findable and downloadable, and existing links and DOIs keep resolving. Registrations and preprints are not part of this change.

This record is a public project and it has a DOI. The copy in this folder was taken on 22 October 2026, while owners can still replace files. Re-run `tools/fetch_osf.py` and compare `docs/osf_manifest.json` before an analysis that depends on the bytes. After 19 February 2027 the public GUID and the DOI should still resolve, and the local hashes are the check that the bytes are the ones logged here.
